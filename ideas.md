## 2. Pilihan Pendekatan: Pendekatan B (Modern Humanis & Reflektif)

- **Design Movement**: Modern Pedagogical Editorial & Clean Humanism
- **Core Principles**:
  1. *Human-Centric Narrative*: Mengutamakan cerita perjalanan dan refleksi daripada sekadar daftar tugas kering.
  2. *Pedagogical Clarity*: Hirarki visual yang jelas untuk memudahkan dosen, guru pamong, dan asesor meninjau dokumen perangkat pembelajaran.
  3. *Informatics Elegance*: Sentuhan teknologi modern yang bersih tanpa kesan futuristik yang berlebihan.
- **Color Philosophy**: 
  - Warna utama Navy Deep (`#0f172a` / `#1e3a8a`) melambangkan profesionalisme dan kedalaman ilmu.
  - Aksen Cyan / Electric Blue melambangkan dunia Informatika dan inovasi digital.
  - Soft Gray dan Pure White memberikan ruang napas yang bersih, menciptakan kenyamanan visual (clean & readable).
- **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric editorial layout untuk halaman beranda dan profil, dipadu dengan card grid interaktif untuk mata kuliah, PPL, dan portofolio.
- **Signature Elements**: 
  - Timeline interaktif perjalanan PPG (Awal hingga PPL).
  - Kartu refleksi berbasis kerangka 6F (Experience, Fact, Feeling, Finding, Future, Action).
  - Badge kategori interaktif dengan animasi transisi halus.
- **Interaction Philosophy**: Responsif, transisi halus (smooth ease-out), umpan balik hover yang jelas pada kartu dan tombol, serta navigasi sticky yang intuitif.
- **Animation**: Fade-in halus saat scroll (`animate-in fade-in duration-500`), hover lift pada card (`transition-transform duration-200 hover:-translate-y-1`), dan tab switching instan.
- **Typography System**: 
  - Heading: Plus Jakarta Sans / Inter (Clean sans-serif dengan bobot bold dan semi-bold).
  - Body: Inter / Open Sans (keterbacaan optimal di berbagai ukuran layar).
- **Brand Essence**: Portofolio reflektif dan dokumentasi profesional calon guru Informatika masa depan yang berdedikasi tinggi terhadap pembelajaran bermakna.
- **Brand Voice**: Reflektif, jujur, profesional, dan inspiratif.
- **Wordmark & Logo**: Monogram tipografi "IHA" (Irvan Hadi Abiyoga) dengan sentuhan ikon kode `< / >` minimalis.
- **Signature Brand Color**: Deep Navy (`#1e3a8a`) & Cyan Accent (`#06b6d4`).

## Style Decisions

The next revision intentionally shifts the portfolio from a dark technology dashboard toward a modern humanist editorial journal. Deep Navy will remain the framing color for navigation, hero moments, and footer, while narrative sections will use Clean White and Soft Gray surfaces for calmer reading. The primary brand mark is now an ownable IHA monogram with a minimal `< / >` gesture, repeated in the navigation and footer instead of relying on a generic graduation-cap logo. Page copy will stay in first-person professional Indonesian and avoid showing bracketed data placeholders; missing personal information will be labeled as data that is still being prepared. The recurring visual thread is a journey path with reflective notebook surfaces and 6F accent colors.

The homepage will use an asymmetric editorial composition: a left narrative column for Irvan's point of view and a right journey panel that previews the PPG path. Supporting sections will alternate soft surfaces and navy frames so the site feels like a thoughtful educator's portfolio rather than a collection of dark cards.
